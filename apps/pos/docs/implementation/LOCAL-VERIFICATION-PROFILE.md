# Local verification

Use [ordinary POS Gradle builds and tests](../../../../docs/pos/BUILDING.md).
The former custom local profile, source-copy/SDK observers, init guards and
receipt machinery are retired. Historical reports remain unchanged in Git history
and their original evidence records; they are not active build prerequisites.

Frozen acceptance expectations and meaningful production persistence/RPC tests
remain required. Compilation is not full business or owner acceptance. Central
implementation and verification remain held pending separate authorization.
