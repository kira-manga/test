## Complete-case prerequisites

The frozen QAM README/QAM-05, APV coverage table, UAD2 README/UAD2-03–05 and
`qa/pos/designs/action-approval-original-history` v1 (OH) define the missing
production boundaries. A complete approval-backed action requires all of the
applicable prerequisites below:

1. Genuine original-native company/branch installation, authenticated local
   employee sessions and admitted devices, actual active Master ownership and
   currently activated employee policies. Every protected boundary rechecks
   authority and serializes revocation with commit; a client cache, ID or signed
   historical fact cannot supply current authority.
2. Genuine own-authentication approval issuance through the authorized flow and
   its returned persisted AP identity. The complete independently bound request
   preserves requester, actual approver, mode, action, operation, document/related
   revisions, semantic content, bounds, reason, scope and original policy versions.
   Separate and the explicit action-specific NamedSelf exception need separate
   positive and refusal controls.
3. Actual single-use consumption/CAS on that exact target operation, integrated
   with the real business handler. No separate data projection establishes
   issuance, freshness, expiry, actor/session grants or successful consumption.
   Fault and duplicate schedules must prove that consumption and the business
   outcome commit together exactly once.
4. Authenticated selected purchasing policy/publication/rule/retention and
   original root/delegation provenance, plus current independently read supplier,
   all SKU/product/catalog/warehouse/tracking sources. Every explicit selected
   coordinate and complete rule must match; an absent source never becomes zero
   generation or an accepting default.
5. Actual native completion and protected retrieval of exact documentary bytes,
   ordered manifests and immutable originals. Supplemental sources require the
   actual completed parent and original attachment-head membership. File hashes,
   media, purpose and supplied IDs alone cannot qualify these originals.
6. One durable coherent custody/business-result/action-use/retention/audit/
   journal/outbox origin, independently inspected through fresh authorized
   persistence views. Every original approval/consumption/result/effect edge
   preserves the original tuple and policy versions. Rejected operations add no
   successful business/consumption/use effects and preserve earlier originals.
7. Real retry/conflict/concurrent-consumer and revocation schedules, lost response,
   supported restart/restore and scoped original-result recovery. An independently
   proved committed result survives later revocation without renewing permission.
   Missing/changed/unsupported joins refuse reconstructed authority; an unproved
   outcome stays unresolved with original evidence retained. An issuance with no
   visible consumption tail never proves that the approval is unused.
8. Protected same-device production composition and genuine admitted local RPC,
   with the required offline, partition, retry, failure and activated-policy
   variants. Actual platform/native-driver/device lanes retain their own NOT_RUN
   status until executed. Required non-device gaps remain development work;
   central services remain held.
